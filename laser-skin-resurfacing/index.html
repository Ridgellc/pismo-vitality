Skin Tightening and Resurfacing

Who doesn't want to have better skin? With Morpheus8 treatments, you can reverse the signs of aging, repair scar and sun damage, and improve skin texture using advanced skin care technology.

Call (805) 773-0707

Morpheus8 revitalization can improve the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles without the downtime or side effects that surgical procedures require.

What is Morpheus8?

InMode Morpheus8 is a minimally invasive skin rejuvenation procedure that pairs micro-needling with radiofrequency (RF) energy.

This FDA-cleared device uses tiny needles to deliver RF energy to different depths of the skin. The needles and heat from the RF energy create micro-injuries, which trigger your body's natural healing response and promote the production of new collagen and elastin, to firm the skin and lessen wrinkles.

The treatment is most commonly used on the face and neck (especially to treat jowls and firm the jawline, under-eye area, and around the mouth), but it can be used just about anywhere on the body, including the abdomen, buttocks, chest, arms or knees.

Morpheus8 treatments can address a wide range of skin concerns:

  • improve the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles
  • firm and tighten mildly sagging skin
  • smooth skin texture
  • reduce the appearance of large pores
  • treat hyperpigmentation and acne scarring
  • help fade stretch marks
  • reduce some fat just below the skin (though not significantly enough to create facial volume loss)
  • For optimal results, most people need a series of up to three treatment sessions, spaced four to six weeks apart.
  • Morpheus8 is a noninvasive procedure that's suitable for all skin tones, with little to no risk of hyperpigmentation.
  • Treatments are quick and relatively painless, with the help of topical numbing cream or local anesthesia.
  • The device penetrates deeper into the subdermal tissues than any other RF micro-needling device, going as deep as 8mm. Its manufacturer says that this allows for more dramatic results at a faster rate than similar treatment options.
  • Needle depth and radio frequency intensity are adjustable, depending on the area being treated and what you want to accomplish. This helps your provider avoid fat loss unless they're specifically targeting excess fat—for instance, when treating a double chin.
  • Treatments can be performed on any body area, for a wide range of skin concerns.
  • Side effects are generally mild, and downtime is minimal, typically consisting of some swelling and redness for a few days.

Morpheus8 can treat a variety of skin conditions. Contact Pismo Vitality today to learn more!
