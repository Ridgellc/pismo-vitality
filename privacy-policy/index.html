Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting the privacy of all visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website. This policy applies only to information collected through our website and not to information collected offline or through other means.

Information We Collect

We may collect non-personally identifiable information through automated technologies such as cookies, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring website addresses

We do not collect personal health information (PHI) through our website.

How We Use the Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Monitor and analyze traffic trends
  • Ensure the security and integrity of the site
  • Support business and website optimization

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to collect non-personal data for site analytics and functionality. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third-Party Disclosure

We may share non-personally identifiable data with trusted third-party service providers who help us analyze website performance and improve services. These providers are obligated to maintain the confidentiality of the data.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
